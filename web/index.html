How to read this ↗
One dot. One peer.

A cluster represents a room. The lines illustrate direct peer connections; they are not measurements of live media.

Live shapes use anonymous room sizes, shared only when a host opts in. No names, links, IP addresses, or locations are published.

Video can travel via peers. At six or more participants, compatible browser groups can forward video from its source through other peers. The stronger branches and traveling dots illustrate one video source; faint links show direct audio. Direct video remains the fallback.

A filled dot is the source. A double ring marks a peer passing video onward. Motion illustrates the design, not actual packets.

In the labeled examples, peers gently join and leave over time. Live shapes change only when the shared room counts change.

Several peers can share an IP address. A dot represents a participant, not a unique IP.
