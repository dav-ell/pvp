TRUST STARTS WITH THE TRUTH

Privacy, in plain language.

A recovery room deserves clear promises. Here’s what this version does, and where trust still matters.

Encrypted between participants.

WebRTC encrypts each peer connection with DTLS-SRTP. Our signaling service exchanges connection information, but doesn’t receive your call media. Compare connection codes through a separate trusted channel to verify peer certificates.

No built-in recorder. No AI meeting notes.

The app has no recording, transcription, or media storage feature. Participants can still record using other software or devices. Group agreements remain essential.

A chosen name, with practical limits.

No account or email is required. We don’t show or export network addresses. Direct P2P still exposes your IP address to peers. Hiding it from peers requires relay infrastructure; hiding it in the interface does not provide network anonymity.

Less infrastructure, not zero infrastructure.

Hosting, signaling, and STUN services help you connect. Signaling sees setup metadata and connection addresses. Meeting titles, creator identities, host sessions, and meeting membership can be stored by the service. Joining guests use temporary sessions. Use a non-identifying group title.

Free to use. Honest about the cost.

Direct media avoids paying a server to carry every conversation. Hosting and maintenance still cost money. Free access is a product commitment, not proof of privacy or a guarantee of perpetual funding.

How is this different from Zoom?

Zoom supports optional end-to-end encrypted meetings. A centralized service is not automatically insecure, and we don’t claim that every meeting is recorded for auditing. Our focus is a simple recovery experience with encrypted direct calls and no built-in recording.
